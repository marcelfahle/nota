## Design Context

### Users

Nota serves independent professionals and small billing teams managing invoices in the flow of client work. They need to create, review, send, collect, find, and export invoices quickly without becoming accounting-software experts. The interface should reduce recall and decision load, surface the next useful action, and keep consequential actions understandable.

### Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and precise. Nota should feel like a trustworthy billing operator: reassuring without being chatty, powerful without looking complicated, and opinionated without getting in the user's way.

### Aesthetic Direction

Refined, light-first utility inspired by shadcn's clarity and composability, guided by Kathy Sierra's focus on making the user feel capable. Use restrained neutral color, crisp typography, quiet borders, compact controls, and deliberate status color. Prefer progressive disclosure over dense always-visible controls. Avoid dashboard theater, decorative gradients, glass effects, excessive cards, and generic AI visual tropes. Support dark-theme tokens where existing components already do, but optimize the current product experience for light mode.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next useful action obvious in context; measure success by what users can accomplish, not how many features are visible.
2. Keep primary actions immediate and place secondary or destructive actions behind clear progressive disclosure.
3. Preserve trust with explicit status, scope, dates, amounts, and confirmations around consequential operations.
4. Favor compact, scannable information density with strong alignment and meaningful whitespace over decorative containers.
5. Meet WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion preferences, and full mobile access to critical actions.
